⚠ Tournament Photos Migration (one-off)

Copies winner.imageUrl into winner.firstWinnerDeck and initializes winner.secondWinnerDeck, winner.thirdWinnerDeck, winner.secondWinnerName, winner.thirdWinnerName and commemorativePhotos.
